Purdue Macro Finder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Purdue Macro Finder (the "Service") is built to work without collecting personal information. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Every page, script, and style is served from the Service's own site, so your browser does not contact any other website while you use it. This policy explains what the Service does with the small amount of data it necessarily handles.

1. Information you provide

When you request a meal, your macro targets, diet and allergen filters, and foods to exclude are sent to the server so it can compute a result, along with either the date and meal periods you chose or a request for what is being served right now. This input is processed in memory to build the response and is not saved afterward or written to the server's logs.

Allergen filters can reveal health information. They are used only to leave foods off your plate and, like the rest of your input, are not stored.

If you select Copy link, your settings, including any allergen filters, are written into a web address that you can share. Anyone who opens that address sees those settings.

2. Information collected automatically

  • IP address. The server temporarily uses your IP address to enforce rate limits, so that no single visitor can overload the Service. Rate-limit counters are held in server memory and are cleared when the Service restarts.
  • Request logs. Standard web server logs may briefly record request details, including an IP address and any error messages, for debugging and abuse prevention. These logs are not used to identify or profile visitors.

The Service does not set cookies. It does use your browser's local storage to remember your settings (macro targets, diet and allergen filters, foods to exclude, meal periods, and whether you chose Right now or Plan ahead) so they are filled in on your next visit. These settings stay on your device and reach the server only as part of a meal request, as described above. Selecting Clear removes them, as does clearing this site's data in your browser.

3. How information is used

The limited data above is used only to operate the Service: to return a result, to keep the Service available by limiting request rates, and to diagnose errors.

4. Sharing

Personal information is not sold, rented, or shared. The Service is hosted on Render, which processes requests on our behalf as part of delivering the site and handles infrastructure logging under its own privacy terms.

5. Third-party data

Menu and nutrition information is retrieved from Purdue University Dining's public API. Your identity is not attached to those requests. This Service is not affiliated with Purdue University or Purdue University Dining.

6. Data retention

Your inputs and generated results are not stored after the response is sent. The Service keeps a local cache of dining hall menu data to reduce load on Purdue's API; that cache contains menu and nutrition information only, and no personal information.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect the Service, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your choices

Because there are no accounts and no personal data stored on the server, there is nothing there to log into or delete. Settings remembered by your browser can be removed at any time with the Clear button. You can stop using the Service at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
